2. The Core Production Workflow Pipeline

SMM PRO coordinates video creation from initial client briefing all the way to social publication across seven automated stages:

1

Client Onboarding & Profiling

Add the client in the Clients tab with brand colors, fonts, tone of voice, USP, target audience, and primary contact person. The system automatically creates a dedicated client room and media folder.

2

Content Creation & Assignment

Create a new Content item (Reel, Carousel, Video, Story) with deadline and priority. Assign dedicated team members (Writer, Shooter, Editor, SMM) or let the system apply the client's default team.

3

Script Writing & Version Control (V1 / V2)

The assigned Scriptwriter receives an automatic task. They write the Hook, Scene breakdown (Dialogue + Visual), CTA, and Music direction. Clicking Submit routes it to the Manager/SMM for review.

4

Secure One-Click Client Approval

Once internal review passes, the system generates a secure, one-time Client Review Link. The client opens the link on their mobile device or desktop to approve or request changes with zero login required.

5

Shooting & Direct-to-Drive Media Upload

Client approval immediately triggers an automated Shooting Task. The videographer shoots footage and uploads raw files straight to Google Drive through resumable chunked streams without burdening the API server.

6

Editing & Timestamped Feedback

Raw footage triggers the Video Editor Task. The editor streams the footage, edits V1, and uploads. The SMM can leave pinpoint feedback at specific timestamps (e.g. 0:14 - swap music track).

7

Final Sign-Off, Scheduling & Publication

The client reviews the final render. Upon final client approval, an automated Scheduling Task notifies the SMM to schedule the post across Instagram, YouTube, or Facebook.

3. Role-by-Role Operating Guidelines

👑 Super Admin & Admin

  • Manage agency settings, roles, and granular permissions.
  • Monitor server health, memory, uptime, and database connection.
  • Configure Google Drive, AiSensy, and Email integrations.
  • View complete audit logs with IP tracking for every action.

👔 Content / Production Manager

  • Monitor the organization dashboard and global team workload.
  • Review scripts and video edits before client delivery.
  • Reassign tasks when a team member is on leave or blocked.
  • Resolve bottlenecks and track deadline compliance.

✍️ Script Writer

  • Open assigned tasks in the My Work tab.
  • Draft scripts with hooks, dialogue, visuals, and timing.
  • Review feedback when changes are requested and submit V2.
  • Access client brand guidelines directly inside the editor.

🎥 Videographer / Shooter

  • Check upcoming shoot deadlines and scene requirements.
  • Upload raw takes directly into the content's media tab.
  • Mark shoot tasks as complete to notify the editor instantly.

✂️ Video Editor

  • Stream raw footage with range-request seeking (fast forward/rewind).
  • Upload V1 render; respond to timestamped SMM change notes.
  • Upload final polished versions (V2/V3) with thumbnail.

📱 Social Media Manager (SMM)

  • Generate and share WhatsApp client review links.
  • Schedule final approved assets across social channels.
  • Record client communications (calls, meetings, feedback).

4. Security & Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

SMM PRO includes bank-grade security controls to safeguard agency content and client assets:

Setting Up Google Authenticator (TOTP OTP)

  1. Download Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator on your iOS/Android phone.
  2. In SMM PRO, navigate to Settings → Profile.
  3. Locate the Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) card and click Enable 2FA.
  4. Scan the displayed QR code with your phone.
  5. Enter the 6-digit code shown in your app to confirm.
  6. Save your 8 backup codes in a secure place. These allow emergency login if your phone is lost.
Brute-Force Protection: The system automatically locks accounts for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive failed login attempts to prevent password guessing attacks.

Large Video Direct Streaming

  • No Server Bottlenecks: Video files up to 2GB upload directly from the editor's browser to Google Drive via resumable sessions.
  • HTTP Range Requests: The video player supports instantaneous seeking and playback without downloading the full video first.
  • Strict Access Control: Drive files are private. Team members can only stream files for content they have permission to view.
